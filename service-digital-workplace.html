Common questions

How do you define the scope for this service?

We start with a discovery discussion to understand business goals, current technology, dependencies, constraints and desired outcomes. We then agree the scope and deliverables before work begins.

Can this service work with our existing technology stack?

Yes. Recommendations are based on the current environment and requirements. We discuss compatibility, integration, security and operational ownership before suggesting changes.

Can Siatech Solutions support both consulting and implementation?

Engagements can focus on assessment and architecture, implementation support, technical enablement or a combination, depending on the agreed scope.

How do we get started?

Contact us with a short description of the challenge, your current environment and the outcome you want to achieve. We can then discuss the most useful next step.
